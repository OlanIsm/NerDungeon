# M09 — Weak Topics & Learning Report

## Goal
Show the learner what they know and what needs review.

## Scope
- topic accuracy
- weak-topic persistence
- strong topics
- post-run report
- answer history feeding mastery

## Acceptance criteria
- weak topics come from real history
- report works after win or loss
- empty/new states are handled
- metrics are understandable

## Stop condition
Stop for review.
