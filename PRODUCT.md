# Nerdungeon

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
android

## Stack
React Native with Expo and TypeScript. Frontend and backend have separate folders.

## Users
Students turning their study notes into fantasy dungeon adventures, primarily on phones.

## Product Purpose
Learn from uploaded PDF/DOCX material through RPG questions and encounters.

## Capabilities and Constraints
The current user-authorized scope is five navigable UI screens with local demonstration interactions. Backend game logic, payments and question generation are outside this UI implementation.

## Brand Commitments
Reproduce the five supplied Stitch screens from project 11414880717679601819: Home, Adventure, Gacha, Inventory, Battle Study. Their supplied artwork and layout are the visual authority.

## Evidence on Hand
HTML and screen images in docs/stitch/nerdungeon-educational-rpg-ui; original hosted artwork downloaded into frontend/assets/stitch with source URL manifest.
